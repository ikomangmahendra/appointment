# Self Check-In via Kios QR scan, alongside manual Resepsionis Check-In

Check-In gains a second channel: a Patient can perform it themselves by scanning a Kode Check-In at a Kios, with no Resepsionis involved. This applies to every Patient with an Appointment, regardless of Booking Source — a Kode Check-In is generated per Appointment at creation time and delivered via WhatsApp for `phone` bookings (extending the existing Deep Link delivery pattern) or shown in Akun Pasien for `online` bookings. Restricting Self Check-In to Patients with an Akun Pasien was considered and rejected: it would make phone-booked Patients permanently second-class for a channel that costs nothing extra to extend to them, since the WA delivery mechanism already exists for Deep Link.

Self Check-In writes Visit Status directly to `menunggu`, the same single-step transition a Resepsionis's Check-In performs — it does not reopen ADR 0007's rejection of an intermediate arrival stage. It's a second way to trigger an existing transition, not a new stage. It only covers the happy path (nothing changed since booking); an Appointment needing exception handling is left for a Resepsionis to check in manually. A Kode Check-In identifies exactly one Appointment, matching the Visit Status Board's one-card-per-Appointment model — a Patient with two same-day Appointments has two codes and scans twice, rather than one scan fanning out to both.

A Kios confirms a scan by showing the Patient's name for the Patient to confirm, with no further identity input. A secondary factor (date of birth, last digits of NIK) was considered and rejected for now: there's no incident motivating it, and it would add friction to every scan to guard against the comparatively rare case of a forwarded code. A Kios accepts a scan at any time within the Klinik's Jam Operasional, with no early-arrival cutoff — matching a Resepsionis's own unrestricted timing, rather than making the unattended channel stricter than the staffed one. If the Appointment is no longer eligible (already resolved by Tutup Layanan, or already past `menunggu`), the Kios shows an error and points the Patient to a Resepsionis; it never reverts or overrides Visit Status itself. Each Kios is bound to exactly one Klinik and never creates an Appointment — a walk-in without one must be registered by a Resepsionis first.

The Appointment now records a Kanal Check-In (`resepsionis` or `kios`) for audit and board display. This doesn't touch ADR 0009: reverting a Check-In stays a Resepsionis action regardless of which Kanal Check-In performed it, since revert is about correcting a front-desk entry, not about who originally triggered the transition.
