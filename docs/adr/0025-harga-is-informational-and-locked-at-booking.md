# Harga is informational only, one fixed value per Layanan, locked into the Appointment at booking

Layanan gains a mandatory Harga (price): a single value per Layanan per Klinik, not varying by Dokter or by Patient category (child/adult, insurance/out-of-pocket) — that kind of tiered pricing is a materially bigger feature nothing else in this domain has hinted at, so it's left for later if ever needed. Harga is purely informational, shown to help a Patient decide whether to book; the Platform doesn't process payment, track payment status, or handle refunds — nothing elsewhere in this domain (no invoice, no payment status) suggests it should, and actual payment continues to happen at the Klinik itself, outside the Platform.

An Appointment locks in the Layanan's Harga at the moment it's created, the same way Booked By is fixed at creation rather than tracking the current state of things. If Admin Klinik changes a Layanan's price later, already-created Appointments keep showing what they were quoted — a Patient shouldn't see the price silently drift before their visit, even with no real payment on the line. Because a reschedule creates a brand-new Appointment (ADR 0003), it locks in whatever Harga is current at that later moment rather than preserving the original booking's price — it's priced the same way any new Appointment is, with no special-cased carve-over.
