# A Jadwal/Pengecualian/Jam Operasional edit that orphans a confirmed Appointment requires an explicit, reasoned force

Editing a Dokter's Jadwal, adding a Pengecualian Jadwal, or changing a Klinik's Jam Operasional can make a Slot that already has a `confirmed` Appointment on it no longer exist. Rather than silently deleting or moving that Appointment, the edit is held pending — the admin sees exactly which Appointments would be affected and must explicitly force it through with a short written reason (captured for the audit log per story #46), or cancel their edit. Forcing it through sets Appointment Status to `perlu_dijadwal_ulang`, a new non-terminal status, rather than `cancelled` or `rescheduled` directly — a person decides which of those two it actually becomes, using the existing Reschedule/Cancel actions; no new resolution mechanism was added for this status.

Forcing is refused outright — not just discouraged, blocked with no override — when any affected Appointment's Visit Status shows the Patient has already arrived (anything other than `belum-tiba`). Once someone is physically at the clinic, reshuffling their booking from the back office no longer makes sense; that has to be handled in person.
