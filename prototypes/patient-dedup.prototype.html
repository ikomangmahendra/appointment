Patient Matching & Patient Merge

A clickable model of the duplicate-patient rules, for anyone to poke at.

Question this answers: ADR-0004 says Patient Matching auto-merges two Patient records only on an exact NIK match, and treats everything else as a Duplicate Candidate that needs manual review by a Resepsionis or Admin Klinik. Does that rule actually feel right once it meets messy, real-looking data — typos, shared household phones, look-alike names, NIK entered late, reviewers who got it wrong? Click around freely below, or run a guided scenario to see the awkward cases on purpose.
The model being tested, in plain terms:
  • Both records have a NIK and it's identical → certain match → auto-merged instantly, no review, no matter what else differs.
  • Both records have a NIK and it differs → treated as definitely different people, even if name/phone/date of birth all look identical.
  • Otherwise, at least 2 of 3 signals must match — phone number, name (small typos tolerated), date of birth — to raise a Duplicate Candidate pending review.
  • Only one matching signal (e.g. phone number alone) → not flagged at all.
  • A dismissed Duplicate Candidate stays dismissed unless a later edit produces a certain NIK match — NIK evidence overrides a human's earlier "not the same person" call.
